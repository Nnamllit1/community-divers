written service code. It grants no new rights and changes no existing licenses.
The service currently remains MIT licensed. The proposed **10% surplus cap**
requires a policy decision; this text has not received legal review. See the
[licensing plan](licensing-plan.md) for the component scope and adoption steps.

## 3. Permitted community funding

You may operate a service without charge. Funding connected to the covered service
is permitted only as voluntary donations or transparent shared hosting-cost
contributions, subject to sections 4 and 5. You may recover the actual cost of
supplying a physical software/source copy; this does not permit selling software
licenses or disguising service charges as distribution fees.

A donation must not purchase access, priority, voting influence, equipment,
progression, cosmetics, commands or any other service entitlement. A simple public
thank-you is permitted with the donor's consent. Shared hosting-cost contributions
may fund ordinary access on disclosed, equivalent terms; they must not purchase a
higher gameplay tier, exclusive content or advantages over other participants.
All funding and related charges must be included in the accounting below.

This version does not authorize sales of pets or other cosmetics, paid bundles,
advertising or sponsorship placements, progression, equipment, mission outcomes,
campaign influence or access to privileged gameplay tools. A future cosmetics
policy would need a separate permission; it is not implied by this draft.

## 4. Costs, reserves and modest surplus

Use a consistent calendar-year accounting period and currency. "Eligible Costs"
are actual, documented costs reasonably attributable to operating the service:
hosting, compute, bandwidth, storage, backups, domains, required infrastructure
services, payment-processing charges and directly attributable transaction taxes.
For owned equipment and shared internet/electricity, count only a reasonable,
documented service share over its useful life or billing period. Do not count the
same expense both as a purchase and as depreciation, or across multiple services.
Deduct refunds, credits and reimbursements for the same expense.

Unpaid or imputed labor, operator compensation, unrelated purchases, development
budgets, personal income tax and inflated related-party charges are not Eligible
Costs. An operator may pay a related party only for a documented eligible expense
at no more than its actual attributable cost; any markup is operator surplus.
Reasonable compensation to the operator comes from the surplus allowance below.

**Proposed limit, not yet approved: operator-retained surplus may not exceed 10%
of that year's Eligible Costs.** Treat every transfer for the benefit of the
operator or a related party that is not an eligible expense or documented repayment
of an expense advance as surplus, whatever its label. Count all commonly controlled
covered services together; moving funds or invoices between them must not increase
the allowance. Do not withdraw more surplus than earned against costs already
incurred, and do not create an entitlement to surplus if funds are unavailable.

Funding above costs and permitted surplus may be held as a separately accounted
reserve for specified, budgeted future service costs. It is not personal profit or
available for unrelated spending. Publish its purpose and reconcile it annually.
Spending a reserve on a future eligible cost counts that cost only in that future
year; depositing money in a reserve is not itself an Eligible Cost. Alternatively,
refund excess funding or reduce future hosting contributions.

On permanent closure, use remaining reserves for outstanding eligible costs, refund
contributors where practicable, or transfer them to another service operating
under these terms with a disclosed continuation plan. An operator cannot take a
closure reserve as extra profit or sell it to itself through another entity.

## 5. Prohibited arrangements and transparency

Do not operate the covered service to offer real-money gambling, paid randomized
rewards, wagering with purchased or redeemable value, or cash-out schemes. Ordinary
random gameplay rewards without purchased stakes or cash-out are not gambling
under this condition. Do not sell gameplay advantages or any entitlement excluded
by section 3.

Do not arrange prohibited funding indirectly through donation tiers, affiliated
stores, third parties, unrelated memberships or bundled purchases. A payment that
buys a prohibited benefit remains prohibited regardless of its description.

Before collecting funding, publish the funding terms and cost categories. Within
90 days after each calendar year, make an aggregate account available to service
users showing receipts, eligible costs, operator surplus and reserve movements and
balance. Keep supporting records for three years. Do not publish donor identities,
credentials or individual financial records. If a Licensor identifies a specific
credible compliance concern, provide reasonably necessary redacted evidence or an
independent accountant's confirmation, protecting personal information.

## Worked funding examples

These examples explain the proposal; they do not make it effective.

| Situation | Result under this draft |
| --- | --- |
| EUR 1,000 eligible annual costs; EUR 1,050 received | Up to EUR 50 may be retained; the cap is an allowance, not a promise of income. |
| EUR 1,000 costs; EUR 1,500 received | Up to EUR 100 retained. The remaining EUR 400 stays in a documented service reserve, is refunded, or reduces future contributions. |
| EUR 0 eligible costs; EUR 200 donations | No operator surplus allowance. Keep the funds for documented future service costs or return them. |
| Operator prepays EUR 300 of documented hosting | Repaying that advance is cost recovery, not surplus; count the underlying expense once. |
| Operator pays themselves EUR 500 for administration | Counts as surplus, not an expense that increases its own cap. |
| Donate EUR 5 to receive a pet or queue priority | Not permitted; this buys an entitlement even if described as a donation. |
| Free pets available to every player | The funding restrictions do not prohibit this. |
| Random ordinary mission loot with no bought stakes or cash-out | The gambling restriction does not prohibit this. |

A restricted license cannot guarantee that operators comply, retract earlier MIT
permissions, control independent implementations, or govern code a Licensor does
not have the rights to license. Review enforceability, scope and compatibility
before adopting this draft.
